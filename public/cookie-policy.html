Cookie Policy

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

As a B2B wholesale furniture supplier, we use minimal cookies to ensure our website functions properly and to understand how visitors interact with our product catalog.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Required for the website to function properly. Enables basic features like page navigation and access to secure areas. Session / Persistent
Analytics Help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. 2 years
Preferences Remember your settings and preferences (like cookie consent choice) for a better browsing experience. 1 year

3. Specific Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

  • cookieConsent - Stores your cookie preferences (1 year)

Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics)

  • _ga - Distinguishes unique users (2 years)
  • _gid - Distinguishes unique users (24 hours)
  • _gat - Throttles request rate (1 minute)

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors engage with our product catalog. This helps us improve our B2B wholesale offerings. All data is processed anonymously.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in various ways:

  • Browser Settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these settings in the "Options" or "Preferences" menu.
  • Our Cookie Banner: When you first visit our site, you can choose to accept all cookies, only essential cookies, or customize your preferences.
  • Third-Party Tools: You can opt-out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. B2B Data Collection Notice

As a B2B wholesale supplier:

  • We do not collect personal data through cookies for individual consumer profiling
  • Analytics data is used solely to improve our product catalog and website functionality
  • We do not share cookie data with third parties for advertising purposes
  • Any business inquiries submitted through our contact form are handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our business operations. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
