1. Introduction

Soinp Gaming ("we," "us," or "our") operates soinp.com as a B2B wholesale platform for gaming furniture and accessories. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or submit business inquiries.

Important: We are a B2B (Business-to-Business) wholesale supplier. We do not sell products directly to individual consumers, and we do not collect personal data for consumer profiling, targeted advertising, or individual marketing purposes.

By using our website or submitting an inquiry, you confirm that you are acting on behalf of a business entity and consent to the data practices described in this policy.

Contact Us: For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at privacy@soinpgaming.com.

2. Information We Collect

As a B2B inquiry platform, we collect only the minimum information necessary to respond to your business inquiries and provide our wholesale services.

When you submit a product inquiry or contact us, we collect the following business information:

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect the following technical information for website operation and improvement purposes:

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, navigation path
  • Network Information: IP address (anonymized), approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • Referral Source: How you found our website (search engine, direct link, referral URL)

This information is collected through cookies and similar technologies. For detailed information, please see our Cookie Policy.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

As a B2B wholesale platform, we do not collect:

  • Consumer purchase history or transaction records
  • Payment information (all payments are handled through secure third-party channels)
  • Personal identification numbers (ID, passport, etc.)
  • Biometric data
  • Sensitive personal data (health, political opinions, religious beliefs, etc.)
  • Data for consumer profiling or behavioral advertising

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Use — Business Inquiry Response

The main purpose of collecting your information is to respond to your wholesale inquiry:

  • Process and respond to your product inquiry within 24-48 hours
  • Prepare and send product quotations tailored to your requirements
  • Provide product samples, specifications, and technical documentation
  • Coordinate order details, logistics, and shipping arrangements
  • Provide after-sales support for confirmed orders

3.2 Secondary Use — Website Improvement

We may use anonymized and aggregated data to:

  • Analyze website traffic to understand which products and categories are most popular
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Optimize our product catalog based on business interest trends

3.3 Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Legitimate Interest: Responding to your business inquiry and providing B2B wholesale services
  • Consent: Your acceptance of this Privacy Policy when submitting an inquiry
  • Contract Performance: Fulfilling our obligations under any purchase agreement
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting tax, customs, and export control obligations

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We respect the confidentiality of your business information. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

4.1 When We May Share Your Information

  • Logistics Partners: Shipping companies (e.g., DHL, FedEx, Maersk) to fulfill order delivery — shared only with your explicit authorization
  • Customs and Authorities: Required for export documentation, customs clearance, and trade compliance
  • Legal Obligations: When required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or reorganization

4.2 International Data Transfers

As an international supplier based in China, your inquiry data may be transferred to and processed in China. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Secure data transmission via SSL/TLS encryption
  • Access restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Data retention limited to the period necessary for the stated purposes

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your business information:

  • Encryption: SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission between your browser and our servers
  • Access Control: Inquiry data accessible only to authorized sales and support staff
  • Server Security: Regular security updates, firewall protection, and intrusion detection
  • Data Backup: Encrypted backups with access logging
  • No Payment Data: We do not process or store any payment card information on our servers

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

7.1 Rights Under GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)

  • Right of Access (Article 15): Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18): Request limitation of how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Object (Article 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7): Withdraw your consent at any time

7.2 Rights Under Other Regulations

  • PIPL (China): Similar rights to access, correct, and delete personal data
  • CCPA (California): Right to know, delete, and opt-out of data sale
  • Other Jurisdictions: Rights may vary; contact us for specific information

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@soinpgaming.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.
